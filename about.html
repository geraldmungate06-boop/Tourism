Our Story

Manicaland State University of Applied Sciences

Manicaland State University of Applied Sciences (MSUAS) is a premier institution of higher learning located in Mutare, the capital of Manicaland Province, Zimbabwe. Founded in 1983, the university has grown from a small technical college into a comprehensive applied sciences institution serving over 5,000 students.

MSUAS is uniquely positioned at the intersection of academic excellence and Manicaland's rich cultural heritage, offering programmes in Applied Sciences, Business, ICT, Environmental Studies, Arts, and Tourism & Heritage Management.

Our campus is more than an educational institution — it is a living heritage site and cultural hub that connects students with the deep roots of Manicaland's history, natural environment, and artistic traditions.

What We Stand For

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide innovative, industry-relevant education in applied sciences while preserving and promoting the rich cultural heritage of Manicaland Province, empowering graduates to drive sustainable development in Zimbabwe and Africa.

Our Vision

To be the leading applied sciences university in southern Africa, recognised for academic excellence, cultural leadership, and the production of innovative graduates who transform communities.
